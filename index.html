Who We Are

A Mission Born from Compassion

Sarvsevak Foundation is a non-profit organisation committed to creating meaningful change in the lives of India's most vulnerable populations. We believe that every human being deserves dignity, care, and the chance for a better tomorrow.

Our work spans 12 core welfare initiatives — from hunger relief and medical assistance to education, women empowerment, and disaster response. Every rupee donated is used transparently and responsibly.

About Sarvsevak
Foundation

A nonprofit born from a deep belief that every human life holds immeasurable worth — and that together, we have the power to transform society.

Our Story

Built on Faith in Humanity

Sarvsevak Foundation was established with one unwavering belief: that no human being should be left behind. In a country of immense diversity and disparity, we saw millions struggling for basic necessities — food, medicine, education, and dignity.

We began with a single food distribution drive, and grew — initiative by initiative, donor by donor, volunteer by volunteer — into a multi-program foundation serving communities across India.

Our name, Sarvsevak, means "servant of all" — a philosophy that drives everything we do. Every program, every donation, every act of service is guided by compassion, transparency, and accountability.

"

Our mission is not merely to provide temporary relief — but to create lasting change that empowers individuals, families, and entire communities.

— Sarvsevak Foundation
What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Every decision we make is guided by these principles that form the heart of Sarvsevak Foundation.

🤝
Compassion

We approach every person we serve with empathy, dignity, and genuine care — treating each individual as family.

🔍
Transparency

Every donation is used responsibly and accountably. We believe our donors deserve full visibility into how funds are deployed.

🌱
Sustainability

We aim not just to provide immediate relief but to create long-term solutions that break the cycle of poverty.

🤲
Inclusivity

We serve all communities without discrimination of caste, religion, gender, or background. Humanity is our only criterion.

💪
Empowerment

We believe in enabling people to become self-sufficient — teaching skills, providing opportunities, and building confidence.

⚡
Urgency

When someone needs help, time matters. We act quickly, efficiently, and without bureaucratic delay to serve those in crisis.

Our 12 Welfare
Initiatives

Comprehensive programs designed to address every facet of poverty — from hunger and illness to education, empowerment, and emergency relief.

01
🍱
Food Support for the Hungry & Needy

Millions of people in India struggle every day to afford even one proper meal. We provide nutritious food to the poor, homeless, daily wage workers, and underprivileged families. No human being should suffer from hunger — your support helps serve meals to those in need.

FoodDaily ReliefHunger
02
🏥
Medical Assistance for the Poor

Many people lose their lives simply because they cannot afford timely medical treatment. We support financially struggling patients by helping them access proper medical treatment, medicines, surgeries, and emergency care. Every donation is used transparently for patient welfare.

HealthcareMedicineEmergency
03
📚
Education Support for Underprivileged Children

Many talented children are forced to discontinue their studies due to financial difficulties. We provide educational assistance, study materials, school fees, scholarships, and guidance — empowering children to rise above poverty and build a better future.

EducationScholarshipsYouth
04
🏠
Support for Orphans & Widows

Orphan children and widowed women face daily hardship without family support. We provide essential help, educational opportunities, daily necessities, and financial assistance — helping them live with dignity, hope, and security.

OrphansWidowsDignity
05
👩‍💼
Women Empowerment Programs

Women are the backbone of every family and society. Through vocational training, small business support, skill development, digital literacy, and awareness campaigns, we help women become financially independent, skilled, and self-reliant.

WomenSkillsIndependence
06
🆘
Emergency Relief & Disaster Support

Natural disasters can destroy lives within moments. We distribute food packets, drinking water, medicines, clothes, blankets, and hygiene kits to families affected by floods, fires, and pandemics. In difficult times, humanity and unity are our greatest strength.

DisasterEmergencyRelief
07
👴
Old Age Support

Many elderly people are abandoned and financially weak. At an age where they deserve comfort and respect, many struggle for food, medicines, and care. We provide essential assistance, medical support, and care programs ensuring every senior lives with dignity.

ElderlySenior CareDignity
08
💧
Clean Water & Hygiene Awareness

Access to clean drinking water and proper hygiene remains a major challenge in rural India. We distribute clean drinking water, support sanitation projects, and spread awareness about personal hygiene and healthy living practices in underprivileged communities.

WaterSanitationHealth
09
👕
Free Clothing Distribution

Thousands of poor families struggle to protect themselves from harsh weather due to lack of proper clothing. We organise clothing drives to provide clothes, blankets, footwear, school uniforms, and essential daily-use items to underprivileged individuals.

ClothingBasic NeedsWinter
10
🛠️
Skill Development & Employment

Many young individuals are willing to work but lack access to training and opportunities. We provide skill development programs, career guidance, vocational training, digital education, and employment support — helping youth build financially independent futures.

SkillsEmploymentYouth
11
🩸
Blood Donation & Health Camps

Many patients lose their lives due to blood shortage and lack of healthcare access. We organise blood donation drives, free medical camps, health checkups, eye camps, and awareness programs — providing basic healthcare without financial burden.

BloodHealth CampsAwareness
12
🐾
Animal Welfare Support

Animals are part of our society and deserve love and protection. Many stray animals suffer from hunger, injuries, and illness. We support animal welfare through feeding drives, rescue support, medical treatment assistance, and awareness programs promoting kindness.

AnimalsWelfareCompassion
